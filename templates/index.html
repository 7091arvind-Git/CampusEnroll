Semester Academic Registration Guidelines

Please review the academic enrollment policies before finalizing your course registrations:

  • Credit Ceiling: Students may register for a maximum of {{ MAX_CREDIT_LIMIT }} credits for the current semester.
  • Seat Availability: Course enrollment is confirmed in real time. Once classroom capacity reaches zero, no additional registrations can be placed.
  • Drop Window: Students can drop enrolled courses before the semester deadline, immediately releasing their seat to other students.
